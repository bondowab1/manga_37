# Hu Tao

![Hu Tao](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/gensin-impact/images/0/0b/Character_Hu_Tao_Full_Wish.png)

**Origin:** Genshin Impact  
**Element:** Pyro  
**Weapon:** Polearm  
**Region:** Liyue

## About

Hu Tao is the 77th Director of the Wangsheng Funeral Parlor, a renowned organization in Liyue that handles funeral rites and related affairs. Despite her young appearance, she takes her work very seriously and has a deep understanding of the cycle of life and death.

She is known for her playful and mischievous personality, often pulling pranks and writing poetry. Her unique worldview blends humor with a philosophical acceptance of mortality.

## Appearance

Hu Tao has long dark brown hair with reddish tips, styled in two braids. Her eyes are a striking reddish-brown with flower-shaped pupils. She wears a black and red qipao-style outfit with a plum blossom ornament on her hat.

## Abilities

- **Normal Attack:** Secret Spear of Wangsheng — performs up to 6 consecutive spear strikes.
- **Elemental Skill:** Guide to Afterlife — consumes HP to infuse her polearm with Pyro and boost her attack.
- **Elemental Burst:** Spirit Soother — commands a blazing spirit to deal AoE Pyro damage; heals Hu Tao based on enemies hit.

## Personality

Hu Tao is cheerful, eccentric, and deeply passionate about her work. She writes poetry about death and the afterlife, which others often find unsettling but she considers beautiful. She genuinely cares for the deceased and their families, ensuring every soul receives a proper farewell.

> *"Don't be afraid. I'm just here to make sure everyone gets a proper send-off."*
